# Show & Swap Notes

> Module 2 · Validation. Put the prototype in front of a peer. Their reaction is the data.

## What I showed

Churn Radar, a clickable CS dashboard that predicts which B2B SaaS accounts are at risk of churning early enough for Customer Success teams to investigate and intervene.

## What my partner understood (in their words)

I understood the hypothesis to be that CS teams can use early customer behavior to identify likely churners before they leave, but the predictions need to be accurate and explainable enough for CS to trust them.

## Where they got confused / pushed back

I understood which accounts were considered high risk, but I wanted stronger evidence that the predictions were trustworthy and a way to see whether previous predictions actually turned out to be correct. The lack of loading, empty, insufficient data, and error scenarios also made the experience feel more like a controlled prototype than a real product.

## What I'll change as a result

Make prediction trust more explicit by improving how probability, confidence, and supporting evidence are communicated, and add realistic product states and a way to validate predicted risk against actual customer outcomes.
