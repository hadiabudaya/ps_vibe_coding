## Show & Swap read, round 2

_A NEW partner, a blind read. What landed differently from v1?_

- **Feels like a real product, or a mockup?** It’s starting to feel like a real product, but still closer to a polished prototype because I don’t yet feel like I’m working with something that responds deeply to my actions.
- **Where interactivity fell short:** I expected to be able to drill into the numbers, investigate why something changed, filter the data, and move from an insight to an action; when those paths don’t go very deep, the dashboard starts feeling staged.
- **Would they show it to a VP?** Not yet. I’d show it as a promising concept, but before calling it a credible solution I’d want to demonstrate a complete story: spot a problem → understand why it happened → quantify its business impact → take or recommend an action.
