## The build

- **What I built:** It’s a customer churn analytics dashboard that gives teams a quick view of churn performance, highlights at risk customers, and surfaces the key factors driving retention so they can take action.
- **Tool used:** Replit
- **Shareable link:** https://churn-dashboard.replit.app

## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** It’s meant to help me understand customer churn and identify which customers are at risk, presumably so my team can intervene before they leave.
- **What confused them:** I’m not immediately sure what I’m supposed to do with the information. I can see churn data and risk signals, but I’m left asking: What caused this customer to become at risk? What should I do next? And how confident should I be in these signals?
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** The prototype seems to be testing the assumption that giving customers visibility into churn and retention analytics will help them recognize the product’s value and give them a reason to keep using it.
